Misc

Escpause menu · release mouse
Creatures Wander passively. Whack them with LMB for meat — cow ×3, sheep ×2, chicken ×1. Watch out: cacti hurt when touched.

Specials

Skateboard (starts in slot 2) RMB to drop it, RMB again nearby to ride — W/S throttle & brake, A/D steer. W+Space ollie — grabbing Shift right after the jump (or holding it first) turns any jump into a trick (W/S pitch, A/D roll mid-air; land flat or get bucked off). LMB ×3 breaks a placed board back into your hand.
MLRS truck (parked near spawn) RMB beside it to take the cockpit: top-down aerial targeting view. Hold LMB to arm, WASD + mouse fly the reticle, release to fire a 12-rock salvo at the bullseye (20 s reload). RMB or Esc exits anytime.
Computer (starts in slot 3) RMB on a floor sets the machine down. RMB it again to sit at its screen: the pointer unlocks and the little desktop OS takes over — move the mouse freely, click Pelican Bench for its app window — an animated SVG of a pelican riding a bicycle — or click Terrainria to play a tiny Terraria-ish side-scroller on screen (A/D move · W/Space jump · S fast-fall; grab the coins and reach the flag; × closes either window). Watch the clock in the taskbar corner. RMB or Esc stands you back out.
